# Cue

Native tvOS media player (SwiftUI + UIKit), a GPLv3 fork of Orivio TV.
Content comes from Stremio add-ons (AIOStreams, debrid configured inside them).
Syncs with a **Nuvio** account (`Core/NuvioAccount/`, QR at first launch).
Bundle id `app.cue.tv`, URL scheme `cue://`, defaults keys `cue.*`.
Server-facing values (`nuvio_tvos_preferences`, `syncSource: "nuvio"`) must not change.

## How we work

- **Quick iterations; the user verifies on the TV.** For look and feel: make the
  change, one Debug build as the compile check, install on the sim, hand over
  with a short note. No screenshot rounds, no "let me verify" loops.
- Verify yourself only for behaviour or focus bugs, or when asked — not
  while a design is still being iterated (focus is made proper after).
  Tool: `Tools/RemoteDriver/remote.sh "<keys>" [launch args]` presses real
  Siri Remote keys on the sim (`up/down/left/right/select/menu`, `hold`,
  `wait:<s>`, `shot:<name>`, `focus`, `<key>*<n>`; ~20 s per run).
- No test targets or UI test suites unless asked.
- **Unsure what the user means? Ask before building.** Many changes start as a
  discussion; when the user says "lets discuss", propose, don't build.
- Keep old code only when told to; otherwise remove what a change makes unused.
- No placeholders: things that belong together appear together.
- No speed/length multipliers for tuning: change the real values.
- Don't reintroduce anything listed under "Removed / rejected".
- Don't read `docs/*.md` unless the user asks — this file is the summary.
  Update this file when a decision changes.

## Build and run

```bash
xcodebuild -project Cue.xcodeproj -scheme Cue -configuration Debug \
  -destination 'platform=tvOS Simulator,id=2DF61D5F-2062-4C19-A952-EB24AC6EA7C3' \
  -derivedDataPath <scratchpad>/dd build 2>&1 | grep -E "error:|\*\* BUILD" | sort -u
xcrun simctl install 2DF61D5F-2062-4C19-A952-EB24AC6EA7C3 <dd>/Build/Products/Debug-appletvsimulator/Cue.app
xcrun simctl launch --terminate-running-process 2DF61D5F-2062-4C19-A952-EB24AC6EA7C3 app.cue.tv
```

- Debug only while iterating. Release only when the user measures fps.
- The project is generated by **xcodegen** from `project.yml`. Regenerating
  wipes Xcode GUI edits to the scheme: put launch args in `project.yml`
  (`schemes.Cue.run.commandLineArguments`), back up and diff the scheme after,
  tell the user. Prefer adding code to existing files over new files.
- `Cue/Secrets.swift` holds real keys and is gitignored — never commit it.
- Dev launch args: `-detailDemo -detailID tt…` (a show; add `-detailMovie`
  for a film — The Two Towers tt0167261 has a collection), `-detailDemoSeries [tt…]`
  (Breaking Bad default; Re:Zero tt5607616, One Piece tt0388629),
  `-transitionSlowMo` (4×), `-detailsHold`, `-holdMenuDemo`, `-browseDemo <person|genre|keyword|company|network> <tmdb id> <name>`,
  `-folderDemo`, `-moviesDemo`, `-homeFocusCollection`, `-cue.home.billboardAutoPage NO`.
- Render Lab (Settings → Performance) holds experiment switches
  (`PerformanceSettingsStore` / `RenderProbe.shared.flags`). Remove options
  once a decision is made.

## Design rules

- **Direction:** flat artwork, glass controls, one outline for focus. Calm,
  quick motion; a stable 60 fps beats fancy.
- The top bar hides by moving out the top and shows by moving back in
  (`GlassSidebar.hidden`, `Motion.move`) — everywhere; it is always in the
  view tree, disabled while hidden.
- **Glass only for what floats above the page** (top bar, hold menus, source
  picker, dropdowns, toasts); `liquidGlass(in:)`, always as a background.
  Everything in the page is flat (`FlatControl`). Details' and the
  billboard's buttons (`DetailActionButton`) and Details' season buttons
  (`FixedFocusSeasonFace`) share one face (`ActionFace`): at rest a
  pill on the top bar's glass (`liquidGlass`, its own edge; the frost —
  the system's thin material, dark, a 1 pt edge brighter along the top —
  read too see-through over bright pictures, kept in the code for now:
  `actionFaceGlass` false; a flat white 16 % lacked contrast; tried: a
  thicker frost, dark and light; the scope pill's flat look; on the
  billboard, flat raised fills — light grey read as light mode, black
  40 % with an edge and shadow — glass is the look over pictures), content in
  the text's colour; focused: a white capsule (the one pure white), dark content,
  5 % larger, soft shadow; in quick (0.15 s), out 0.35 s; pressed: shrinks to 90 % (0.1 s, at least that long for a quick tap), back on a no-bounce spring (0.3 s) (`DetailActionButton.pressScale`; 97 % went unnoticed) — over a
  resting pill the capsule fades into it (same size: shrunk, two pills
  showed); with nothing at rest it shrinks into the button (88 %) as it fades, both from the start, the
  fade even (a delayed fade overlapped the next capsule); the content
  blends along with it (tried: a quick switch at half the fade).
  Seasons: only the row's season on a pill — the flat light grey
  (`FlatControl.selected`, the current one of a set; the blur looked
  odd there) — the others plain, muted. Text semibold, icons bold, 26 pt (the buttons 64 tall).
  The first button labelled — its name alone, no icon (tried: with its
  icon); searching, a spinner in the name's place, same width — (billboard:
  Details; Details: Play), the rest
  icon-only (billboard: Library, Next — only its chevron, no pill;
  Details: Library, Trailer, About), their names under them while
  focused. (Tried: bold text; every button labelled.) (Tried and dropped: a dark fill
  at rest — grey, black, black with a light edge; nothing at rest.)
- No accent colours. Colour only for information (rating sources).
- Text: 24 pt regular is the base; 23–25 pt is the floor for TV text.
- Text levels (dark mode, after tvOS's own grey text): nothing is pure
  white — primary white 85 % (`FixedFocusText.primary`, theme
  `textPrimary`, titles), secondary 60 %, tertiary 45 %. The system font
  (SF Pro). Badges on coloured fills stay white.
  Row names: 40 pt semibold, white 55 % — as dim as tvOS's own heading
  (measured on its empty-page title; `FixedFocusText.heading`). (Tried:
  its size and weight too, 48 pt regular.)
  The info under the cards (the fixed box's, the captions): names 70 %
  (out of focus 60 %), the second line 50 % (`FixedFocusText.caption…`;
  tried: as the row names, 55 / 45 / 40 — hard to read).
  Folder and browse pages' headers alike: the page's name as the row
  names (55 %), a person's bio 70 %, the kind label, facts and subtitle
  50 %.
  Details' About the same: the summary, the facts and the chips' names
  70 %, the small labels 50 %; the Summary panel's text 85 %.
- Motion tokens (`Motion`): focus 0.3 s, move 0.35 s, present 0.45 s
  (no-bounce springs), fade 0.25 s. One press, one movement.
- Animate transforms and opacity only. Never alpha 0 on a focusable list
  (tvOS skips transparent views); hide cell contents or mask instead.

## Decided mechanisms

- **Home rows are UIKit**: native focus, scrolling off; we move rows with Core
  Animation in `didUpdateFocus`. Fixed box at the focus spot for most rows;
  collections, Continue Watching and Saved for Later have moving focus (the
  row stays, focus moves across the cards — grown 5 % — a caption under
  each). The fixed rows' posters right of the box also get a
  caption (name, year — at rest's levels) while their row has focus, as
  moving-focus rows (`FixedFocusPosterCell.caption`; none under the box,
  left of it, or in rows out of focus — no extra room between rows).
  Going it fades out in 0.08 s (`captionFadeOut`; it rode the move's
  own duration a long way), coming back in as its mirror: over the
  same share of the way (the spring's first 0.08 s covers ~40 %), at its
  end (`captionFadeIn`; tried: the last 0.12 s — too late). The poster
  going under the box or coming out (its caption travels the box's width
  and more): the first and last 30 % of the way only
  (`grownFadeShare`; with the other captions' fades — ~40 % — it showed
  too much movement beside the box's barely drifting info; tried: 10 %
  — too little; 25 %).
  The box's content changes as one movement with the row: on its spring,
  the captions' timing (`FixedFocusBoxView.show`). The info drifts with the
  picture (~14 pt sideways, 8 up/down), crossfading over the whole move
  on the row's spring — the crossfade matters most. (Tried: 30 pt, the
  last commit's — much better than on the captions' timing; as a caption, on the captions' timing — moving a poster's
  step, `pitch`; on the path of the caption going under the box, ~750 pt
  — much better, maybe too far; 500 pt; 180 pt — half the captions'
  speed; with the picture, ~14 pt.) A caption sits under its poster wherever that is in its cell
